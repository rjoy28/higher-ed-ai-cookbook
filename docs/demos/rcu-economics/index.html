RCU Program Economics

The Portfolio Explorer sized Riverside Christian University's programs by headcount. Headcount is not revenue. This page joins three recipe inputs that usually sit apart: program enrollment, tuition and aid by segment, and the fall section schedule. The rules that join them are settings, not facts, so they are on the page where you can change them.

Rules

Try

Aid is reported by segment, not by program, so every program in a segment gets the same discount unless you give online students their own rate. Moving an online rate moves aid between online and campus students; the segment's total aid stays the same.

Largest by headcount, largest by net tuition

Programs in the top 12 on either list, ranked both ways. A line that climbs to the right is a program that brings in more than its size suggests.

Sticker price and net tuition

Per student, by segment, from the aid summary. The dashed line is the sticker price. The solid line is what the university keeps after institutional aid.

Every program

Select a column heading to sort. Net tuition is allocated from the segment totals by the rules above, so it reconciles to the aid summary but is an estimate for any one program.

Share of seats taught against share of net tuition

Each program's share of enrolled seats in this term's sections, against its share of net tuition. Programs on the diagonal teach in proportion to what they bring in. Hollow marks at the left edge have no sections on record.

Gaps in the join

Before reading anything into the numbers, check what did not join. A program with no sections looks like it earns revenue for free. It does not; its teaching is recorded somewhere else, or not at all.

Program teaching load

Seats per major above 1 means the program teaches students from other majors, usually through general-education or service courses.

Sections to review

The Course Section Viability recipe's flags, with the program each section joins to.

What this page does not do. It has no cost data. Seat share is not cost, and net tuition per program is an allocation. The page shows where revenue and teaching sit and which rules put them there. It does not say which programs pay for themselves.

In the recipes, an AI assistant drafts recommendations with evidence-basis tags, and people decide. A real version of this join needs the full term schedule, credit hours by major, and instructional cost, in the same source file.

The source

This YAML is the whole demo. Change a headcount, remap a subject, or edit the rules, then select Apply to re-render every view. Edits stay in this browser tab until you copy them out.

segments
Recipe 17's aid summary: headcount, gross tuition, institutional aid, and funded aid per segment, one value per year.
programs
Recipe 01's input. Each program's level must name a segment, and program headcounts must add up to the segment's, every year.
sections
Recipe 16's section export for the term.
subjects
The join. Each course prefix names the program it belongs to, or null for a course that serves several programs. Add a note when a mapping needs explaining.
settings
The rules. The sliders start here, and Recipe defaults returns to these values.

Riverside Christian University is a fictional fixture institution, and its data is synthetic. It matches the sample outputs of the Academic Portfolio Analyst, Net Tuition & Discount Analyzer, and Course Section Viability recipes.
