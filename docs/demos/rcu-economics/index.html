RCU Program Economics

The Portfolio Explorer sized Riverside Christian University's programs by headcount. Headcount is not revenue. This page joins three recipe inputs that usually sit apart: program enrollment, tuition and aid by segment, and the fall section schedule. The rules that join them are settings, not facts, so they are on the page where you can change them.

Revenue rules

Try

Aid is reported by segment, not by program, so every program in a segment gets the same discount unless you give online students their own rate. Moving an online rate shifts aid between online and campus students and reorders the programs. Each segment's total aid stays the same.

From the aid summary

These are segment totals. The rules decide how they are split across programs, so they do not move with the online sliders. The discount goal changes only the color of the rate.

Largest by headcount, largest by net tuition

Programs in the top 12 on either list, ranked both ways. A line that climbs to the right is a program that brings in more than its size suggests.

Sticker price and net tuition

Per student, by segment, from the aid summary. The dashed line is the sticker price. The solid line is what the university keeps after institutional aid.

Every program

Select a column heading to sort. Net tuition is allocated from the segment totals by the rules above, so it reconciles to the aid summary but is an estimate for any one program.

Section rules

Try

Flags for this term's sections, and how far a program's share of seats can drift from its share of net tuition before it is flagged.

Share of seats taught against share of net tuition

Each program's share of enrolled seats in this term's sections, against its share of net tuition. Programs on the diagonal teach in proportion to what they bring in. Hollow marks at the left edge have no sections on record.

Gaps in the join

Before reading anything into the numbers, check what did not join. A program with no sections looks like it earns revenue for free. It does not; its teaching is recorded somewhere else, or not at all.

Program teaching load

Seats per major above 1 means the program teaches students from other majors, usually through general-education or service courses.

Sections to review

Sections flagged under-enrolled, over cap, or near cap, with the program each one joins to.

Read with care. There is no cost data here. Seat share is not cost, and net tuition per program is an allocation. These views show where revenue and teaching sit and which rules put them there, not which programs pay for themselves.

A full answer needs the whole term schedule, credit hours by major, and instructional cost, joined to the same programs.

Scenario

Try

Set a discount rate for the latest year and say how many students you expect to lose for each point it drops. The break-even line needs no guess: it is how many students could leave before the change costs money.

Net tuition change as undergraduates leave

The change in total net tuition at the scenario rates, for each share of undergraduates lost. Left of the break-even line, the scenario brings in more than today. The marker shows your enrollment-response setting.

Programs under the scenario

Each program keeps its share of its segment, so the order changes only when one segment moves more than the other. The twelve largest by scenario net tuition.

What this model leaves out. It applies the new rate to every student in the latest year at once. Real discount changes reach new students first and take four years to reach everyone, while current students keep their awards. It also treats students who leave as average students, when the ones who leave are often the most price-sensitive and the most aided.

The enrollment response is a setting, not a forecast. Use your own yield history, or test a range. The break-even line is the one number here that needs no guess.

Behind the page

This page joins three Cookbook recipes: Academic Portfolio Analyst (program enrollment), Net Tuition & Discount Analyzer (tuition and aid), and Course Section Viability (sections). In the recipes, an AI assistant drafts recommendations with evidence-basis tags, and people decide.

Source file

Every view on this page is drawn from it

This YAML is the whole page. Change a headcount, remap a subject, or edit the rules, then select Apply to redraw every view. Edits stay in this browser tab until you copy them out.

segments
Recipe 17's aid summary: headcount, gross tuition, institutional aid, and funded aid per segment, one value per year.
programs
Recipe 01's input. Each program's level must name a segment, and program headcounts must add up to the segment's, every year.
sections
Recipe 16's section export for the term.
subjects
The join. Each course prefix names the program it belongs to, or null for a course that serves several programs. Add a note when a mapping needs explaining.
settings
The rules and the scenario. The sliders start here, and Defaults returns to these values. scenario_discount null keeps today's rate.

Riverside Christian University is a fictional fixture institution, and its data is synthetic. It matches the sample outputs of the Academic Portfolio Analyst, Net Tuition & Discount Analyzer, and Course Section Viability recipes.
