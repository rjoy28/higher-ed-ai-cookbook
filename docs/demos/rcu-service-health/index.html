Every category

Select a column heading to sort. "Short of target" is how many more tickets would have had to close inside their SLA for the category to reach the on-target line.

Read with care. Attainment shows where to look, not why. A low category can mean a staffing gap, a vendor, a misrouted queue, or an SLA set too tight for the work.

Behind the page

This page applies the IT Service Health Analyst recipe to a synthetic export. In the recipe, an AI assistant runs the pre-flight check, flags what it finds, and waits for a person to confirm each decision before scoring. It then drafts recommendations tagged research-backed, industry practice, or analyst judgment. The CIO decides.

A case study series on building a ticket health snapshot for a real service desk will draw on this page.

Every view on this page is drawn from it

This YAML is the whole page: the export as received, the pre-flight decisions, and the bands. Edit a ticket, add an alias, or confirm another minutes row, then select Apply to redraw every view. Edits stay in this browser tab until you copy them out.

tickets
The export as received, one row per ticket, with the row number from the export file.
preflight
What a person found and confirmed for each check. Counts and row numbers on the page are computed from the tickets.
canonical, aliases
The labels the analysis expects, and the old labels a person confirmed map to them.
settings
The SLA bands and which checks are on. Defaults returns to these values.
