Behind the page

This page applies the Retention Risk Analyst recipe to a synthetic cohort table. In the recipe, an AI assistant reads retention by entry cohort, returns a scorecard and the gap to goal, and drafts group-level supports tagged research-backed, industry practice, or analyst judgment. Student-success leadership decides.

The recipe never accepts or produces student rows, risk scores, or predictions about who will leave. That keeps the work out of individual education records under FERPA. The source file below holds only group totals, and the page hides any group too small to report safely.

Source file

Every view on this page is drawn from it

This YAML is the whole page: the cohort table, the same cohorts split by group, gateway-course grades, and the goal. Add a cohort, a group, or a course, then select Apply to redraw every view. Edits stay in this browser tab until you copy them out.

cohorts
One row per entry cohort: size, students retained to the second fall, and gateway DFW rate. A cohort not yet observed is null.
dimensions
The same cohorts split into groups. The page checks that every split adds up to the cohort row.
gateway
First-year enrollments and DFW grades by course. They reconcile to the cohort DFW rate.
events, settings
Recorded changes that explain a shift, and the goal bands and smallest reportable group.

Riverside Christian University is a fictional fixture institution, and its data is synthetic. It matches the sample output of the Retention Risk Analyst recipe, and its cohort sizes match the Enrollment Funnel recipe.
