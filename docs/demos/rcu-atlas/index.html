System of record, by stage

Where the source and reality disagree

Each claim in the source names a fact source. This view checks the claims against telemetry snapshots and flags what has gone stale, what has stopped, and what exists without being documented. The fixture's telemetry is simulated.

The source

This YAML is the whole atlas. Change a claim and select Apply to re-render every view. Edits stay in this browser tab until you copy them out.

systems
What exists. Layer, owners, vendor, and runs_on for the platform underneath.
interfaces
What moves. Direction, kind (data or sso), method, frequency, owner, criticality, compliance, failure mode.
fact_source + last_verified
Provenance on every interface. Where the claim can be checked, and when someone last checked it.
domains
System of record for each kind of data, by lifecycle stage.
observations
Telemetry snapshots the claims are checked against. Each names its fact source and capture date.
scenarios
Named starting points for the impact radius.
