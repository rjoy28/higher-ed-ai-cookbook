Where the source and reality disagree

Each claim in the source names a fact source. This view checks the claims against telemetry snapshots and flags what has gone stale, what has stopped, and what exists without being documented. The fixture's telemetry is simulated.

The source

This YAML is the whole atlas. Change a claim and select Apply to re-render every view. Edits stay in this browser tab until you copy them out.

lifecycles
One track of stages per kind of person: student and employee.
systems
What exists. Layer, owners, vendor, runs_on for the platform underneath, and signin when people get in some way other than SSO.
interfaces
What moves between a pair of systems. Kind (data or sso), method, frequency, owner, criticality, compliance, failure mode.
carries
The fields on an interface. Each flows from → to unless it sets flow: back or flow: both, and can carry its own frequency, compliance and provenance.
fact_source + last_verified
Provenance on every interface, field override and sign-in fact. Where the claim can be checked, and when someone last checked it.
domains
System of record for each kind of data, by lifecycle stage. Domains name their lifecycle.
observations
Telemetry snapshots the claims are checked against. Each names its fact source and capture date.
scenarios
Named starting points for the impact radius.
